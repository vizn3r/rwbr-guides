# Suggested Operator Positions

The game is designed for 2-3 players minimum. A single player can manage with auto systems, but earns fewer points. For full manual operation the recommended split is:

- **Reactor and recirculation:** 1 person
- **Turbine main panel:** 1 person
- **Turbine local panel (U2 TCR):** 1 person. If no TCR operator is present, the main turbine operator should walk down to the turbine at least every 10 minutes.
- **Electrical panel:** monitored by the turbine operator and the shift supervisor, no dedicated operator needed
- **CST, Deaerator, Condenser:** 1 person
- **Feedwater:** 1 person

## Shift supervisor

Sits in the Supervisor Room. Monitors both units, watches the demand display, coordinates between MCR operators, and patrols the plant. Senior supervisor and above can grant a unit interlock between U1 and U2 if both are running. See [Supervisor Room](#) for details.

```note
The supervisor role is not strictly defined. The main idea is to monitor, coordinate, and give advice to the MCR operators. Supervisors should not be stuck in the room. Patrol the area, check on both units, and walk the turbine hall.
```

## Inspector

Distinct rank (Inspector+) that performs **Inspections**: a tablet-driven plant walk-around earning 1000 pts (split 500/unit) every 30 minutes. See [Inspection](#).

## Rank prerequisites

Some positions require a certain rank to access:

- Standard control rooms: any rank
- U2 MCR / TCR / FWP / Condenser CR: **5000 U1 points**
- Supervisor Room: **Junior Supervisor**
- Inspector Office + Tablet: **Inspector** rank
- Demand Manager (changing demand split): **Supervisor**
- PA system + Unit Interlock: **Senior Supervisor**

See [Roles & Badges](#) for the full progression.
