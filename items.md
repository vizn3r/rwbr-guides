# Items & Equipment

Items live in your toolbar. Some have practical applications (dosimeters, malfunction tags, cell phones); others are cosmetic or quality-of-life.

## Cards

Every player gets a Card on join. It opens doors via card scanners. If your rank is too low for a given area, a denied sound plays and the door stays shut.

| Restriction | Areas |
|-------------|-------|
| No restriction | U1 MCR, Turbine Hall, Deaerator Hall |
| 5000 U1 points | U2 MCR, U2 TCR, Condenser Control Room, FWP Bay, EDG Bay |
| Junior Supervisor+ | Supervisor Room |
| Junior Inspector+ | Inspector Office |
| Plant Manager | Plant Manager Office |
| RBWR Supporter | Supporter Offices |
| Various private | Administrator, Developer, Instructor, Director offices |

## Dosimeters

Measure player radiation exposure in **µSv** (microsieverts) or **mSv** (millisieverts) depending on type.

### Non-disposable dosimeter

- Given to every player in the toolbar on join
- Activates when passing the dosimeter **entrance gate**, resets at the **exit gate**
- Tracks elapsed time + exposure dose in µSv since entering
- Beeps when radiation rises rapidly. Left-click the tool to mute (left-click again to unmute).
- Manufactured by "Barra Scientific" per the in-game labeling

### Disposable dosimeter

- Range 0-4000 mSv
- Yellow film strip darkens with cumulative dose; darker = higher
- No activation needed
- Pick up from a blue basket near the radiation scanner
- Dispose in the trash bins at the turbine hall entrance

## Handheld Geiger Counter

Two versions:

### Standard (gauge)

- Free, found in every control room
- Analog gauge, **maxes at 500 µSv/h**, useless in heavily irradiated zones
- Added in V1.4 as a primitive free version

### Gamepass (digital)

- Digital display in µSv/h
- **Beeps at 31 µSv/h** or above. Left-click the tool to mute (light goes red when muted; takes ~2 s to silence).
- Added with the Turbine Hall update

## Cell Phones (V1.4.2+)

- Obtained at **Junior Supervisor** rank
- Auto-generated phone number, unique per server (displayed on the phone screen)
- Same functional applications as desk phones, but mobile
- **Do Not Disturb** button stops incoming calls
- Decline a call → caller has a short cooldown before re-calling that number (anti-spam)
- Nokia 3310 model with the iconic ringtone
- A setting allows you to hide your number from the Supervisor Room's phone-list screen

## Inspector Tablet

- Obtained at **Inspector** rank
- Used to conduct an [Inspection](#). See that page for the checklist and procedure.

## Malfunction tags

- **Yellow tags:** apply to a suspected faulty device (from a tag holder on the central desk)
- **Red tags:** affixed by the technician on devices confirmed broken
- Tagging a working device gives 0 points for the repair when it returns
